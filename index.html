Settings
Brush
First tap loads
How much colour one tap on a pan gives. Holding and swirling in the pan keeps loading beyond this.
Brush runs out as you paint
On: water and paint leave the hairs as you draw, so a long stroke fades and ends in dry-brush. Off: the brush holds its load, and every line comes out the same however many you draw.
Pencil pressure → brush size
Apple Pencil force drives the width of the stroke. Off: the Size slider alone decides.
Pressure range
How much Apple Pencil force changes the width of the stroke. At the far right a feather touch draws a hairline and a hard press more than twice the Size, so one stroke can go from thin to thick.
Pencil pressure → paint flow
Pressing harder lets paint leave the brush faster, so a firm pass is darker and a light one a tint. The brush still pays for every bit it lays. Off: pressure changes only the width.
Pencil tilt shapes the brush
A leaning Pencil lays its hairs along the paper: the footprint stretches in the direction of the barrel, up to nearly three times as long. Upright, it is round.
Finger paints
Fingers pinch to zoom, drag to pan and tap with two to undo. Whether one finger also paints: by default it does until an Apple Pencil touches the sheet, after which the Pencil paints and the hand navigates.
Water & paper
Paper
Changing the paper starts a fresh sheet.
Drying speed
How fast the sheet loses its shine. Left = minutes of open wet-in-wet time, right = sets in seconds.
Paint and water run off
On: washes spread, blooms travel, tilted paint runs downhill. Off: the sheet still wets and dries, but colour stays exactly where you put it.
Tilt strength
How hard the paint runs for a given slope. With tilt on, wet paint runs toward whichever edge of the screen is lowest, as on a real board; lay the device flat and it stops.
Salt grain size
Fine table salt through to coarse rock salt. Bigger crystals scatter more sparsely and open larger starbursts.
Performance
Detail
Battery and heat, most to least: Most detail → Medium → Least. Medium does about half the work of Most, and Least about a third of it. What changes is how finely the water is simulated and how large the picture is drawn — the painting behaves the same either way. Pick Least if the device gets warm.
