Drying speed
How fast the sheet loses its shine. Left = minutes of open wet-in-wet time, right = sets in seconds.
Paint per dip
How much colour one tap on a pan gives. Holding and swirling in the pan keeps loading beyond this.
Water per dip
How much water one tap on 💧 gives. Hold and swirl for more.
Salt grain size
Fine table salt through to coarse rock salt. Bigger crystals scatter more sparsely and open larger starbursts.
Paint and water run off
On: washes spread, blooms travel, tilted paint runs downhill. Off: the sheet still wets and dries, but colour stays exactly where you put it.
Brush runs out as you paint
On: water and paint leave the hairs as you draw, so a long stroke fades and ends in dry-brush. Off: the brush holds its load, and every line comes out the same however many you draw.
Pencil pressure → brush size
Apple Pencil force drives the width of the stroke. Off: the Size slider alone decides.
